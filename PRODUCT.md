# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Home buyers, sellers, investors, and relocators across the DMV — primarily Northern Virginia (Fairfax, McLean, Vienna, Alexandria). Clients range from first-time buyers who need patient guidance to seasoned investors comparing options. They arrive with varying levels of familiarity with the process and a shared need to trust the team they choose.

## Product Purpose

The Yusufi Team website generates leads and builds trust for a family-run real estate team operating under Key Realty Group in Fairfax, VA. Principal Broker Akhtar Yusufi has been licensed since 2000; his children Imal (Agent) and Zermina (Director of Operations) complete the team. Success means visitors leave with confidence in the team's expertise and take one action: call, email, or fill out a form.

## Brand Personality

Trusted · Family · Refined. Warm but credentialed — the combination of a family business you can rely on and a team with 25+ years of professional depth. The tone should feel like a knowledgeable friend in real estate, not a transactional portal.

## Anti-references

- **Zillow / Redfin / large portals**: Impersonal, data-heavy, marketplace aesthetic. No grid of thousands of listings, no algorithm-forward UI, no "search-first" framing. This site leads with the people, not the inventory.
- **Generic franchise templates**: RE/MAX, Coldwell Banker boilerplate. No clip-art photos, no default color schemes from a brand kit, no generic market-area stock photography as the lead visual.

## Design Principles

1. **People first, listings second.** The team — their faces, names, credentials, and story — are the primary asset. Listings are evidence of their work, not the lead.
2. **Credentialed warmth.** Every design choice should feel like it came from a family that takes their craft seriously: refined without being cold, personal without being casual.
3. **Earn the call.** Every section answers one question the visitor hasn't asked yet. The page should end with the visitor trusting the team enough to reach out — not just aware they exist.
4. **Restraint over spectacle.** Gold and black carry the brand. Don't compete with the palette; let space and typography do the work.
5. **One action per screen.** Avoid burying calls-to-action in content. Each section has one clear next step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum throughout. 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Reduced motion respect added to any future animation work.
